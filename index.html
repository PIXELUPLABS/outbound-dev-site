How it works

One API. Unlimited possibilities.

Follow one request from search to reply: heads of sales at US fintech companies with 50 to 200 people.

A worked example. You ask for heads of sales at fintech companies in the US with 50 to 200 people. outbound.dev finds 20 people, then 18 work emails and 5 phone numbers; the 2 lookups that find nothing are free. A two-step email and LinkedIn sequence goes out from your own connected accounts once you approve it, and the first reply comes back. The lookups cost less than the free $1 a new account starts with. Sending and reading replies are free; connected inboxes and LinkedIn accounts are billed every 30 days.

FAQ

Questions

Something else? Write to hello@outbound.dev.

What is outbound.dev?

A pay-as-you-go service for outbound. It finds people and companies, gets their work emails and phone numbers, runs email and LinkedIn sequences from your own accounts, and collects the replies. You run it from an AI agent or your own code, and pay from a prepaid balance.

How does pay as you go work?

You add money to a balance, from $10 at a time. Each result is charged against it at the listed price: a person found, an email found, an account connected. There are no plans, no seats and no minimums.

What does a lookup that finds nothing cost?

Nothing. Emails and phone numbers are charged only when one is found.

Do I need a developer?

Not to use it. outbound.dev is an API, so something has to call it, and an AI agent that can make web requests, such as Claude Code, can do that from a plain request. You need a developer only to build it into your own product. Your keys, balance and usage are in the console.

Which inboxes and LinkedIn accounts can I connect?

Any mailbox that offers SMTP and IMAP access, which most email providers do. A LinkedIn account connects through a hosted sign-in page: the account owner signs in there, and we never see or store the password.

What happens when my balance runs out?

Paid calls stop until you top up, and nothing is ever charged twice. Switch on auto-recharge and it will not happen.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Your first organization starts with $1 of usage and needs no card. At list prices that is 10,000 people found or 50 work emails.
